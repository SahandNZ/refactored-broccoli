Do the models' medians predict the sign of the return better than climatology does on the same decisions?

Primary metric: hit-rate difference, the sign hit rate of the median minus climatology's.

**Q1**: no. 0 of 120 cells (118 with a centre test) of the main family (models with at least 3 scored quarters wholly after release) have a median that predicts the sign better than climatology: none; 23 predict it worse (interval below 0). Late family (its own Holm correction and verdict, no): 0 of 75 cells: none. Cells not scored (1, in the table with their status): 1 not scored: 169 decision times, fewer than the bootstrap block 236.

Limitations: test folds; only decisions after each model's release, so a model released inside the window has fewer quarters (late models form their own Holm family and may not meet the 3-quarter condition); one-sided tests at 0.05, intervals unadjusted; the PIT is seen only at the nine forecast quantiles; the rank IC is pooled over the cell's assets and times; decisions where climatology's median is exactly zero (assets with many zero training returns) leave the centre test, so it rests on fewer decisions of those markets (signed_decisions; owner's decision, 2026-11-17); the reflection check (1/P) is not run; the run on the development folds is dev-2026-11-17-c (results/dev-2026-11-17-c/ and the git history of this README)
