## Q2 (RQ-closures)

Is the result of Q1 consistent with an effect of the representation rather than of the market?

Primary metric: CRPS of the first 3 bars after an artificial closure, crypto.

**Q2**: no. 10 of 11 models have the same best representation on crypto with its weekends cut as on the closed markets; different: tirex-1.1 (closed markets trading hours only; crypto missing). Invalid forecasts on the control, which remove the decision from every representation of the model: chronos-bolt-small forward-filled 3; chronos-bolt-base forward-filled 9; chronos-2 trading hours only 2; chronos-2 forward-filled 82; chronos-2 missing 1; tirex-1.1 forward-filled 326; moirai-2-small forward-filled 184; moirai-2-small missing 80. Decisions dropped from every arm for fewer than 32 own closes in the trading-hours context: crypto, weekends cut: 0.

Limitations: the best representation by mean CRPS without a test or an interval (an exemption from experiments rule 13, README Plan changes), so a best inside the noise counts; the control is the 8 crypto assets at one weekly time, the 48-hour weekend cut with decisions on Mondays 00:00-02:00 UTC (the exchange schedule's time), while the real closures are also nights and holidays at many times of day; Toto 2.0's shorter trading-hours contexts are left-padded with their first value to a multiple of its patch (32)
